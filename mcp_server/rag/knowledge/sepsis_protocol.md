# Sepsis Early Recognition and Management Protocol
**Source:** Hospital Clinical Excellence Committee | Version 4.2 | 2024-01

## 1. Definitions

**Sepsis (Sepsis-3, Singer et al. JAMA 2016)**
Life-threatening organ dysfunction caused by a dysregulated host response to infection.
Identified by: suspected infection PLUS SOFA score ≥2 above baseline.

**Septic Shock**
Sepsis with circulatory, cellular, and metabolic abnormalities.
Criteria: vasopressor requirement to maintain MAP ≥65 mmHg PLUS lactate >2 mmol/L despite fluid resuscitation.

**SIRS Criteria** (≥2 required for SIRS; note: SIRS alone ≠ sepsis)
- Temperature >38°C or <36°C
- Heart rate >90 bpm
- Respiratory rate >20/min or PaCO2 <32 mmHg
- WBC >12,000/mm³ or <4,000/mm³ or >10% bands

## 2. qSOFA Bedside Screen (SOFA surrogate)

Score 1 point each:
- Altered mentation (GCS <15)
- RR ≥22/min
- SBP ≤100 mmHg

**qSOFA ≥2 = high risk of poor outcome** → full SOFA assessment, escalate immediately.

## 3. NEWS2 Sepsis Correlation

NEWS2 ≥5 = increased risk of sepsis and 30-day mortality. Initiate sepsis pathway.
NEWS2 ≥7 = urgent clinical review required within 30 minutes.
Any single NEWS2 parameter scoring 3 = immediate medical review.

## 4. Vital Sign Thresholds — Sepsis Screen

| Parameter | Alert Threshold | Action |
|-----------|----------------|--------|
| Temperature | >38.3°C or <36°C | Sepsis screen + cultures |
| Heart Rate | >90 bpm (sustained) | Consider sepsis if febrile |
| Respiratory Rate | >20/min | Blood gas, sepsis screen |
| SBP | <100 mmHg | IV access, fluids, cultures |
| MAP | <65 mmHg | Vasopressor consideration |
| SpO2 | <94% | O2 therapy, consider sepsis |

## 5. Sepsis Six Bundle (first 60 minutes)

1. Give high-flow O2 (target SpO2 ≥94%)
2. Take blood cultures × 2 (before antibiotics if possible)
3. Give IV antibiotics (within 1 hour of recognition)
4. Give IV fluid bolus (30 mL/kg crystalloid if hypotensive or lactate ≥4)
5. Measure lactate and check FBC, U&E, LFTs, CRP, clotting
6. Monitor urine output (catheterise if not already; target ≥0.5 mL/kg/h)

## 6. Early Warning Score Escalation

| NEWS2 Score | Risk | Response |
|-------------|------|----------|
| 0 | Low | Reassess 12h |
| 1–4 | Low–medium | Reassess 4–6h, inform nurse in charge |
| 5–6 or any 3 | Medium | Urgent review within 30 min, consider HDU |
| ≥7 | High | Emergency review <10 min, consider ICU |

## 7. Hemodynamic Targets in Septic Shock

- MAP ≥65 mmHg (vasopressors: noradrenaline first-line)
- Lactate normalisation (target <2 mmol/L)
- Urine output ≥0.5 mL/kg/h
- SpO2 ≥94% (or PaO2 ≥60 mmHg)
- ScvO2 ≥70% if PAC in situ

## 8. Antibiotic Guidance (empirical)

Community-acquired sepsis (unknown source): piperacillin-tazobactam 4.5g IV q6h
Hospital-acquired sepsis (>48h admission): meropenem 1g IV q8h + vancomycin
Neutropenic sepsis: meropenem 1g IV q8h

Always take cultures BEFORE antibiotics if <15 min delay. Never delay antibiotics >1h.

## 9. Monitoring Frequency During Sepsis

Every 30 minutes: HR, BP, RR, SpO2, GCS
Every 1 hour: urine output, temperature
Every 4 hours: lactate, repeat cultures if febrile
Every 6 hours: full vital signs review, SOFA rescore

## 10. Escalation Contacts

- Sepsis ≥ medium risk: Senior Nurse + Registrar within 30 min
- Sepsis HIGH risk / septic shock: ICU Registrar + Consultant within 10 min
- Activate "Sepsis 6 Trolley" at bedside for all HIGH risk patients
